相对位置的指针变换
令角频率  \(\omega_i=\dfrac{1}{10000^{2i/d}}\)
$$\begin{aligned} \textcolor{#7dd3fc}{\mathrm{PE}_{(pos,\,2i)}}&\textcolor{#7dd3fc}{{}=\sin\dfrac{pos}{10000^{2i/d}}=\sin(\omega_i\cdot pos)}\\[6pt] \textcolor{#f6a960}{\mathrm{PE}_{(pos,\,2i+1)}}&\textcolor{#f6a960}{{}=\cos\dfrac{pos}{10000^{2i/d}}=\cos(\omega_i\cdot pos)} \end{aligned}$$
只知道位置编码 \(\mathrm{PE}(\mathbf{?})\)，能否得到间距 \(k\) 的编码 \(\mathrm{PE}(\mathbf{?}+k)\)？
\[\begin{aligned} &\begin{bmatrix}\cos\omega_i(pos+k)\\[2pt] \sin\omega_i(pos+k)\end{bmatrix}\\[12pt] &=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\omega_i\,pos\cdot\textcolor{#6aa0ff}{\cos\omega_i k}-\sin\omega_i\,pos\cdot\textcolor{#6aa0ff}{\sin\omega_i k}\\[4pt] \sin\omega_i\,pos\cdot\textcolor{#6aa0ff}{\cos\omega_i k}+\cos\omega_i\,pos\cdot\textcolor{#6aa0ff}{\sin\omega_i k}\end{bmatrix}\\[12pt] &=\textcolor{#6aa0ff}{\begin{bmatrix}\cos\omega_i k & -\sin\omega_i k\\[2pt] \sin\omega_i k & \cos\omega_i k\end{bmatrix}} \begin{bmatrix}\cos\omega_i\,pos\\[2pt] \sin\omega_i\,pos\end{bmatrix} \end{aligned}\]
\(\mathrm{PE}(\mathbf{?}+k)=\textcolor{#cfe0ff}{R_i(k)}\cdot\mathrm{PE}(\mathbf{?})\)
只知道位置编码 \(\mathrm{PE}(\mathbf{?}_1)\) 和 \(\mathrm{PE}(\mathbf{?}_2)\)，能否知道位置间隔 \(\mathbf{?}_1-\mathbf{?}_2\)？
\[\begin{aligned} &\begin{bmatrix}\cos\omega_i\,pos_1\\[2pt] \sin\omega_i\,pos_1\end{bmatrix}\cdot \begin{bmatrix}\cos\omega_i\,pos_2\\[2pt] \sin\omega_i\,pos_2\end{bmatrix}\\[12pt] &=\cos\omega_i\,pos_1\cdot\cos\omega_i\,pos_2+\sin\omega_i\,pos_1\cdot\sin\omega_i\,pos_2\\[12pt] &=\cos\bigl(\omega_i\,pos_1-\omega_i\,pos_2\bigr)\\[12pt] &=\textcolor{#6aa0ff}{\cos\bigl(\omega_i(pos_1-pos_2)\bigr)} \end{aligned}\]
\(\mathrm{PE}(\mathbf{?}_1)\cdot\mathrm{PE}(\mathbf{?}_2)=\textcolor{#cfe0ff}{\cos\bigl(\omega_i(\mathbf{?}_1-\mathbf{?}_2)\bigr)}\)
正弦编码在点积计算中产生的问题
\(q_m=W_Q\bigl(x_m+\mathrm{PE}(m)\bigr)\)
\(k_n=W_K\bigl(x_n+\mathrm{PE}(n)\bigr)\)
\[\begin{aligned} q_m\cdot k_n=q_m^{\mathsf{T}}k_n &=\bigl(W_Q(x_m+\mathrm{PE}(m))\bigr)^{\mathsf{T}}\bigl(W_K(x_n+\mathrm{PE}(n))\bigr)\\[7pt] &=(x_m+\mathrm{PE}(m))^{\mathsf{T}}\,W_Q^{\mathsf{T}}W_K\,(x_n+\mathrm{PE}(n)) &\textcolor{#8b8b96}{\small(AB)^{\mathsf{T}}=B^{\mathsf{T}}A^{\mathsf{T}}}\\[7pt] &=(x_m+\mathrm{PE}(m))^{\mathsf{T}}\,W\,(x_n+\mathrm{PE}(n)) &\textcolor{#8b8b96}{\small\text{记 }W=W_Q^{\mathsf{T}}W_K}\\[7pt] &=\bigl(x_m^{\mathsf{T}}+\mathrm{PE}(m)^{\mathsf{T}}\bigr)\,W\,(x_n+\mathrm{PE}(n)) &\textcolor{#8b8b96}{\small(A+B)^{\mathsf{T}}=A^{\mathsf{T}}+B^{\mathsf{T}}}\\[7pt] &\mathrlap{=x_m^{\mathsf{T}}Wx_n +\textcolor{#ff9d9d}{x_m^{\mathsf{T}}W\,\mathrm{PE}(n)} +\textcolor{#ff9d9d}{\mathrm{PE}(m)^{\mathsf{T}}Wx_n} +\textcolor{#f3d17a}{\mathrm{PE}(m)^{\mathsf{T}}W\,\mathrm{PE}(n)}} \end{aligned}\]
✗
交叉项污染：因为正弦编码是做加法，点乘结果的中间两项带入了位置的绝对信息
✗
投影矩阵破坏：因为正弦编码是在投影之前被加进去的，投影矩阵会干扰位置编码的计算

把旋转位置编码带入点积计算
\(q'_m=\mathrm{RoPE}(m)\,(W_Q\,x_m)\)
\(k'_n=\mathrm{RoPE}(n)\,(W_K\,x_n)\)
\[\begin{aligned} q'_m\cdot k'_n=(q'_m)^{\mathsf{T}}k'_n &=\bigl(\mathrm{RoPE}(m)\,(W_Qx_m)\bigr)^{\mathsf{T}} \bigl(\mathrm{RoPE}(n)\,(W_Kx_n)\bigr)\\[7pt] &=(W_Qx_m)^{\mathsf{T}}\, \textcolor{#7dd3fc}{\mathrm{RoPE}(m)^{\mathsf{T}}} \textcolor{#f6a960}{\mathrm{RoPE}(n)}\,(W_Kx_n) &&\textcolor{#8b8b96}{\small(AB)^{\mathsf{T}}=B^{\mathsf{T}}A^{\mathsf{T}}}\\[7pt] &=(W_Qx_m)^{\mathsf{T}}\, \textcolor{#7dd3fc}{\mathrm{RoPE}(-m)} \textcolor{#f6a960}{\mathrm{RoPE}(n)}\,(W_Kx_n) &&\textcolor{#8b8b96}{\small\mathrm{RoPE}(m)^{\mathsf{T}}=\mathrm{RoPE}(-m)}\\[7pt] &=(W_Qx_m)^{\mathsf{T}}\, \textcolor{#cfe0ff}{\mathrm{RoPE}(n-m)}\,(W_Kx_n) &&\textcolor{#8b8b96}{\small\mathrm{RoPE}(-m)\,\mathrm{RoPE}(n)=\mathrm{RoPE}(n-m)}\\[7pt] &=\textcolor{#7dd3fc}{q}\cdot \bigl(\textcolor{#cfe0ff}{\mathrm{RoPE}(n-m)}\,\textcolor{#f6a960}{k}\bigr) \end{aligned}\]
✓
交叉项消失：旋转位置编码是做乘法，点乘结果不会产生无意义的交叉项
✓
投影矩阵不再插手：旋转矩阵直接作用在 \(q\)、\(k\) 向量上，不会被投影矩阵影响

编码定义
\(\begin{aligned} \mathrm{PE}_{(pos,\,2i)}&=\sin(\omega_i\cdot pos)\\[3pt] \mathrm{PE}_{(pos,\,2i+1)}&=\cos(\omega_i\cdot pos) \end{aligned}\)
\(\mathrm{RoPE}(pos,\,i)= \begin{bmatrix}\cos(\omega_i\,pos) & -\sin(\omega_i\,pos)\\[2pt] \sin(\omega_i\,pos) & \cos(\omega_i\,pos)\end{bmatrix}\)

运算对象

\(q\)、\(k\) 的算式
\(\begin{aligned} q_m&=W_Q\bigl(x_m+\mathrm{PE}(m)\bigr)\\[3pt] k_n&=W_K\bigl(x_n+\mathrm{PE}(n)\bigr) \end{aligned}\)
\(\begin{aligned} q'_m&=\mathrm{RoPE}(m)\,(W_Q\,x_m)\\[3pt] k'_n&=\mathrm{RoPE}(n)\,(W_K\,x_n) \end{aligned}\)
